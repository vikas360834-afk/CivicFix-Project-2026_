## 3. Abstract
Rapid urban growth across metropolitan centers has made responsive municipal grievance redressal a critical governance challenge. Citizens frequently encounter civic infrastructure failures such as potholes, garbage overflows, non-functional streetlights, water pipeline leaks, and blocked storm drains. However, traditional reporting channels lack real-time transparency, municipal officials suffer from fragmented ticket backlogs, and field engineers struggle with manual dispatch and unverified resolutions. 

**CivicFix** provides a comprehensive, full-stack web platform addressing these operational bottlenecks through a strict three-role architecture: **Citizens (`USER`)**, **Administrators (`ADMIN`)**, and **Ward Engineers (`ENGINEER`)**. Reports undergo an auditable 5-stage lifecycle: `PENDING_APPROVAL` ➔ `OPEN` (admin approved) ➔ `ASSIGNED` (dispatched to engineer) ➔ `IN_PROGRESS` (field work started) ➔ `RESOLVED` (signed off with verification photo and resolution note), or `REJECTED` (with mandatory administrative feedback). 

The platform features an automated NLP category and priority classifier, an interactive Leaflet map featuring marker clustering, heatmaps, and duplicate coordinate jitter, and a conversational AI assistant that queries live database aggregates. A role-scoped analytics dashboard renders real-time KPIs and interactive visualizations (category, status, ward distribution, and 6-month trends). Built on React 18, Node.js, Express, and Prisma with SQLite, CivicFix enhances civic accountability, shortens resolution turnaround, and establishes a data-driven model for municipal governance.

### Operational Workflow:
1. **Citizen Submission:** A citizen submits an issue with title, description, ward, location, GPS pin, and up to 5 photos. An NLP service automatically suggests the category, priority, and municipal department. The report enters the system as `PENDING_APPROVAL` (hidden from the public feed and map).
2. **Administrative Verification & Dispatch:** Administrators access the Pending queue, review photos, check duplicate warnings (flags issues within ~100m submitted in the past 14 days), and either approve the report (moving it to `OPEN`), approve & dispatch directly to a ward engineer (`ASSIGNED`), or reject it with a recorded reason (`REJECTED`).
3. **Field Engineering Resolution:** Assigned ward engineers review their Kanban workspace (`/engineer`), navigate to the site via Google Maps integration, mark work as `IN_PROGRESS`, and sign off on completion (`RESOLVED`) by submitting a resolution note and an after-repair photo.
4. **Public Tracking & Auditability:** The public feed and map display active issues (`OPEN`, `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`). Citizens track their submissions in their Profile (`/profile`) and view an immutable timeline of every status transition generated by `IssueStatusHistory`.
5. **AI Chatbot & Analytics:** A persistent AI widget answers natural-language queries (e.g., open counts, backlog age buckets, ward comparisons, SLAs) by querying live database aggregates.

## 5. Methodology & Implementation

1. **Iterative Requirement Engineering:** Defined strict role boundaries (`USER`, `ADMIN`, `ENGINEER`) and legal lifecycle transitions, enforcing `409 Conflict` on invalid status leaps.
2. **Database Modeling:** Designed relational models in Prisma with foreign-key constraints and indexes on `status`, `ward`, `category`, and `createdAt`. Introduced `IssueStatusHistory` for audit trails.
3. **Security Architecture:** Enforced bcrypt password hashing, stateless JWT authentication, CORS origin restrictions, Helmet HTTP security headers, and rate limiting (login and AI chat). All data endpoints require authentication (`401 Unauthorized` without token).
4. **Geographical & Visual Intelligence:** Implemented Leaflet mapping with chunked marker clustering (`maxClusterRadius: 45`, `disableClusteringAtZoom: 16`), duplicate coordinate jitter, and conditional heatmap/ward-ring overlays.
5. **Conversational AI Service:** Restructured backend AI into a modular pipeline (`knowledge.js`, `context.js`, `queries.js`, `intents.js`, `formatter.js`) verified by 56 automated test cases.
6. **Emerald Glassmorphism UI:** Developed an accessible UI design system utilizing CSS variables for light/dark modes, liquid morphing blobs, and WCAG AA contrast compliance.

## 6. Conclusion & Future Scope

CivicFix proves that integrating modern web technologies with intelligent data routing can solve civic transparency and operational bottlenecks. By isolating public feeds to active issues, providing field engineers with focused work orders, and supplying administrators with duplicate warnings and city-wide analytics, the system establishes end-to-end municipal accountability.

**Future Scope:**
- Integration with municipal GIS shapefiles for sub-ward polygon boundaries.
- Push and SMS notifications via WhatsApp Business API / Twilio.
- Computer vision model to detect duplicate pothole photos automatically upon upload.
- Native mobile companion application (React Native / Android SDK).
